v1.1 - 2026-10-16
----------------------------------------------------------
On 16 Oct 2026, after discussion with fording, several GUI issues were fixed:

1. confirm_payment_prototype_1.1.html (Confirm Payment page)
   - Payment Information section no longer shows Min Points Required.
     It is only shown in the Product section.
     (Min Points Required is a product attribute, not payment info)
   - Removed Available labels from the payment method section
     (no use case step support)

2. my_points_prototype_1.1.html (My Points page)
   - Currency symbol changed from CNY (¥) to EUR (€)

3. points_mall_prototype_1.1.html (Points Mall page)
   - Removed stock quantity / status labels from product cards
     (Available / 3 left / Sold Out) to simplify the page

4. admin_prototype_1.1.html (Admin page)
   - Product management changed from on/off-shelf to Create / Update / Delete (CRUD)
